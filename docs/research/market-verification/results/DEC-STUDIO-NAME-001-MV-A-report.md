## Method and decision boundary

All 60 existing Source IDs linked to the 30 MV-A records were reopened over the live network. Accessibility, final URL/redirect behavior, entity identity, claim support, publisher, origin group, primary classification, and pair independence were reviewed separately. Existing source records were retained even when invalid. No new source IDs were needed or created.

VERIFIED_ACTIVE was assigned only where both existing sources were live-accessible, supported the entity's current public game-industry activity, came from different publishers and origin groups, and included an official/primary source. Same-company pages, controlled sites, blocked pages, broken links, incomplete redirects, and entity-mismatched URLs were not treated as independent qualifying evidence. All other records remain STATUS_UNCERTAIN under fail-closed review.
